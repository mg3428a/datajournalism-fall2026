This grouped column chart compares the types and number of reported (bias) crimes across Washington, D.C.’s wards from 2012 to 2026. I picked this chart to show differences in offenses such as simple assault, threats, and property damage while also showing which wards experience the highest and lowest numbers of reported incidents. In my final project, this visualization will help either prove or disprove how bias crime patterns vary by location and identify which types of offenses are most prevalent in each ward.
