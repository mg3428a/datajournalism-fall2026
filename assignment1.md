# DC Renters are Evicted Every Year

Renters in D.C. facing eviction are some of the most protected tenants compared to almost anywhere else in the country. The proper procedure of evicting tenants consists of: 
1. Landlords hiring private process servers
2. Process servers delivering a summons
3. The summons informing the tenant of the eviction notice
4. The process server completing an affidavit of service
