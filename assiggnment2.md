# Endangered Species Dataset
https://fis.fws.gov/ecp/report/adhocCreator?catalogId=species&reportId=species&utm_&columns=%2Fspecies@cn,sn,status,desc,listing_date&sort=%2Fspecies@cn%20asc;%2Fspecies@sn%20asc

The U.S Fish and Wildlife Service created this dataset as part of their Environmental Conservation Online System. The data contains information about different species managed by ECOS under the Endangered Species Act. This include their common name, scientific name, ESA listing status, entity description and ESA listing date. The database includes both U.S. and foreign species. It also shows  categories beyond endangered species, such as threatened species and species of concern. The database however does not provide a simple, consistent field explaining the primary threat facing every species, which is the information I am adding through outside research. Geographic information also has limitations. FWS notes that "current range of species entities consisting of one or more populations does not include states or countries outside those population boundaries, even though the species may occur there in a population not covered by the Endangered Species Act". The database can also contain species managed by the National Marine Fisheries Service when FWS has those data.


# What are the most common threats a species protected under the Endangered Species Act may face?

The major threats facing each species, such as habitat loss, hunting, pollution, invasive species, or climate change helps an audience understand whether endangered species share common threats and whether certain human or environmental factors appear repeatedly across different species. This information is useful because the original dataset tells readers which species are endangered and when they were listed, but it does not clearly tell them why those species remain at risk. Repeatedly, human disturbance contributes to the endangerement of many of these species whether directly or indirectly.
