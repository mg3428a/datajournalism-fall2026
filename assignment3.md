# Cleaning and Analyzing Data
I wanted to see which clusters had the most crimes and, what the crime category was. This meets the standard of newsworthiness because by looking at what area and what crimes it could potentially help with what to look out for or what areas to avoid.

To create this Pivot table: 
1. I selected Insert then pivot table
2. I selected Neighborhood Cluster and moved it to columns
3. I selected offense and moved it to values
4. I changed offense to be "count of offense"
5. I selected offense key and moved it to rows
The cluster that had the most crime was cluster 11 with 997 total crimes and property theft being the most perputuated crime in that area with 676 cases.
